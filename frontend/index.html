The Tech Pipeline

A rigorous automated workflow that processes sparse weather data into high-resolution terrain insights.

01. Acquisition

Every 3 hours, we fetch live weather data via the Open-Meteo API, covering the entire Tirol region.

02. Interpolation

Sparse station data is projected onto a 100m grid using K-D Tree spatial indexing and IDW interpolation.

03. Snowpack Model

A 4D physics engine integrates snowfall, melt, compaction, and solar shading over time.

  • Atmospheric Physics

    We don't just interpolate; we adjust for the moist adiabatic lapse rate (~6.5°C/km) and apply orographic enhancements based on terrain exposure.

  • Shadow Mapping

    Precomputed binary rasters adjust shortwave radiation based on solar zenith and azimuth angles, pinpointing "cold North faces" with surgical precision.

  • SQH Visualization

    Our Snow Quality Heatmap combines depth and quality. Bright pink opaque splotches indicate deep, cold powder—the holy grail of skiing.
